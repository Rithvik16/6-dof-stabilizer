2. REAL-WORLD 6-DOF MULTI-BODY DYNAMICS ENGINE
- Replaced the simplified static deflection heuristic with a sub-stepped 6-DOF rigid-body physics solver running 8 integration sub-steps per frame (dt = 0.002 s).
- Modeled realistic platform mass and principal moments of inertia (m = 38 kg, I_xx = 2.4 kg*m^2, I_yy = 2.4 kg*m^2, I_zz = 3.8 kg*m^2).

- Projected individual actuator restoring forces into the 6-DOF platform wrench at every physics sub-step using the live kinematic Jacobian transpose (J^T * f) and combined dynamic spring-damper forces with the exact 6x6 Gaussian-elimination quasi-static balance.
- Added full 6-DOF external load controls: Vertical Load (F_z), Lateral Shear Forces (F_x and F_y), Pitch Moment (M_y), Roll Moment (M_x), and Yaw Torque (M_z).

3. JOINT SNAP THRESHOLD DETECTION AND RED FAILURE HIGHLIGHTING
- Added an interactive Joint Snap Limit (F_max) slider (600 N to 2,000 N, default 1,100 N) and a Test Joint Snap preset button to evaluate structural overload conditions without disconnecting the joints.
- Implemented three-tier structural stress states across all 12 joints, clevis collars, and telemetry bars:
  * Nominal State (|f_i| < 82% of F_max): Metallic slate bearings with green (PUSH/compression) or cyan (PULL/tension) telemetry readouts.
  * Yield Warning State (82% <= |f_i| < 100% of F_max): Amber joint and collar glow with WARN telemetry badges and a high-stress HUD alert.
  * Joint Snap Failure State (|f_i| >= F_max): Pulsing crimson red highlight on the overloaded base and platform ball joints, clevis collars, actuator barrel, and 3D failure halo rings, paired with a critical SNAP! telemetry alert.
- Added real-time Structural Integrity HUD and Kinematics HUD displaying peak leg force (N), minimum Safety Factor (SF = F_max / max(|f_i|)), vertical heave deflection (dZ in mm), lateral shear displacement (dX/Y in mm), platform tilt angle (degrees), and joint gap error (0.000 mm).

4. CLEAN 3D VIEWPORT PRESENTATION
- Removed all 3D floating arrow and vector indicators (the 6 actuator counter-force arrows, the external load vector arrow, and the overturning moment ring) for an unobstructed view of the hexapod mechanism and joint stress states.
